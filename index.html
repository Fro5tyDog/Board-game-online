    How to play
    • Modes. Story is a 10-level Day chapter that unlocks plants as you go. Versus pits a Plants player against a Zombies player. The Shop sells upgrades for coins earned in Story. The Level editor builds your own levels. The Almanac lists every plant and zombie.
    • Goal. Survive every wave and clear the final wave. Each row has one lawnmower that fires once if a zombie reaches it. If a zombie gets past a used mower, you lose.
    • Start of turn. You get 25 sun from the sky, and seed packet and Glove cooldowns go down by 1.
    • Your turn. As often as you can afford: plant a seed packet, shovel a plant away (free), move a plant with the Glove (50 sun, 2-turn cooldown), or buy an upgrade (Repeater, Gatling Pea, Twin Sunflower, Tough Bark).
    • Seed prices. There's no waiting on seed packets. Each extra copy of a plant you buy before that packet recharges costs 25 sun more. The red badge shows the extra cost and how many turns until the price drops back.
    • Cards. On turns 4, 7, 10 and so on, three face-down cards are dealt. Look over the lawn first, then press Open your cards. About 2 in 3 cards are hazards and 1 in 3 is a buff. Your first reveal is free. Each extra reveal costs 50 sun, and so does rerolling a revealed card. You must take one card before ending the turn. The others go back into the deck.
    • Taking a card. A hazard resolves when you end the turn. A buff must be used straight away or stored. Stored buffs can be used on any later turn.
    • End of turn. Your hazard (if any) resolves, then plants act, then zombies act.
    • Plants act on timers. Peashooters fire every turn. Sunflowers make 25 sun every 2 turns, and the orange badge counts down. Wall-nuts soak bites.
    • Zombies. Most move one tile every 2 turns. The Flag Zombie moves every turn. A zombie next to a plant bites it every turn instead of moving.
    • Waves. The first wave arrives after turn 3. Each later wave comes once half of the current wave's health is destroyed. After the last wave, the final wave pushes every zombie forward a tile and brings in all remaining zombies.

    How versus works
    • Simultaneous turns. Both players plan their turn at the same time without seeing each other's moves, then lock in. When both are locked in, the turn resolves: plant moves, then new zombies arrive, then plants act, then zombies act.
    • Plants work like the single-player game: sun from the sky, seed packets, Shovel, Glove and upgrades. Survive to the end of the last turn to win.
    • Zombies move a tile every turn in versus, and earn brains every turn (more as the match goes on). Spend them to send Browncoats, Coneheads, Bucketheads and Flag Zombies into any row you choose. Get a zombie past a row whose lawnmower is gone to win.
    • No event cards in versus. The zombie player is the hazard.
